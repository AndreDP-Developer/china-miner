MAKE THE MINE YOUR OWN

Settings

Off · Modern

Steer left or right during a jump, or release to stop. Space alone jumps vertically. A short input buffer catches jumps pressed just before landing. Spikes and lasers require visible contact.

On · Hardcore

Original jump direction lock, timing, character-cell hazards and fall penalties. Hold a direction with Space to jump. Five lives, original scores and original rooms.

Enemy contact follows the original visible sprite pixels in both modes. Changing mode starts the current room fresh with five lives and zero score.

BUILT ON A CLASSIC

In the footsteps of Ian Gray.

Original programming and game design: Ian Gray.
Published by Interceptor Software in 1984 for the Commodore 64. Original music by Chris Cox, based on Scott Joplin’s Maple Leaf Rag.

This fan remake gives Wally’s jade mines a new Three.js presentation. All 30 room maps, enemy paths and original movement routines are retained in Hardcore mode. Enemy sprite masks are tested against Wally’s original sprite pixels at his current position. Modern mode adds air steering and more forgiving jump input; choose your mode in Settings.

The original is tough from the first room: short, deliberate movements matter. Move slightly left before jumping over the first creature. In Hardcore, hold Space with a direction to jump; use ↑ + Space for a vertical jump. In Modern mode, Space alone jumps and you can steer in the air. High falls, spikes, creatures and active lasers are lethal.

The developer tools are for testing. Normal play starts with five lives, resets the room on death, and awards an extra life every five completed rooms. Audio here is newly synthesized ambience and effects.

Fidelity note: original movement is retained in Hardcore; both modes use corrected visual enemy contact. Browser scheduling and sprite-collision sampling do not emulate the entire C64’s raster timing or sound interrupts. Cycle-exact longplay equivalence has not been certified.
